Reason for Denial:
Based on the clinical information provided, this procedure has been determined to be Not Medically Necessary under the terms of your plan (Policy # P-2024-KNEE).

Specifically, Apex Health Clinical Guidelines (Section 4.2: Arthroscopic Knee Surgery) require documentation of at least 12 consecutive weeks of failed conservative treatment prior to surgical intervention for a degenerative meniscal tear. Conservative treatment includes:

Activity modification (Rest).

NSAID pharmacological management.

Doctor-supervised Physical Therapy.

Your records indicate that while you completed 8 weeks of physical therapy, you did not meet the 12-week threshold required to rule out non-surgical recovery. Therefore, the criteria for surgical intervention were not met.
