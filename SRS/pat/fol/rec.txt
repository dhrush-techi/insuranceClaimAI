Left knee arthroscopy with partial medial meniscectomy and chondroplasty. (CPT: 29881)

ANESTHESIA: General Laryngeal Mask Airway (LMA).
TOURNIQUET TIME: 35 Minutes.

INDICATIONS:
The patient is a 45-year-old male presenting with a 4-month history of left knee pain, locking, and mechanical catching. MRI dated 08/22/2025 confirmed a complex tear of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus. Patient has subjective complaints of the knee "giving way." Patient has failed 8 weeks of physical therapy and NSAID management.

OPERATIVE FINDINGS:
Examination under anesthesia revealed no ligamentous instability (Lachman negative, Pivot shift negative). The arthroscope was introduced through the standard anterolateral portal.

Patellofemoral joint: Articular cartilage intact. Tracking central. No loose bodies.

Medial compartment: A complex, unstable flap tear of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus was identified, flipping into the joint space. This correlated with the patient's mechanical symptoms. There was also Grade III chondromalacia (fibrillation and fissuring) on the weight-bearing surface of the medial femoral condyle measuring approximately 1.5cm x 1cm.

Lateral compartment: Lateral meniscus intact. Popliteal tendon intact.

Intercondylar Notch: ACL and PCL intact and well-tensioned.

DESCRIPTION OF PROCEDURE:
The extremity was prepped and draped in the usual sterile fashion. A tourniquet was inflated to 250 mmHg. Standard portals were established. Diagnostic arthroscopy was performed as above.

The unstable meniscal fragments in the medial compartment were resected using arthroscopic biters and shavers until a stable, balanced rim was achieved. Great care was taken to preserve the meniscal capsule. The chondral defect on the medial femoral condyle was smoothed using a shaver (chondroplasty) to prevent further delamination.

The joint was irrigated copiously with 3L of saline. The knee was ranged through a full range of motion, and no impingement was noted. Portals were closed with 3-0 nylon interrupted sutures. Sterile dressings were applied.

PLAN:
Weight bearing as tolerated with crutches for comfort. Physical therapy to commence in 3 days. Follow up in clinic in 2 weeks for suture removal.
