✅ 1. WHAT ARE “METRICS” IN YOUR PROJECT?

⚠️ Important:
This is not a single ML classifier, so accuracy/precision alone is NOT enough.

Your system is a decision + reasoning pipeline, so metrics are multilayered.

🔹 A. Reasoning & Decision Metrics

(How good is the AI’s decision?)

Mean confidence score

% AUTO_DRAFT vs NEEDS_REVIEW

Evidence sufficiency rate

Category-wise confidence average

🔹 B. Evidence Quality Metrics

(How strong is evidence mining?)

Avg evidence count per case

Evidence density (evidence / document length)

Evidence type distribution

Avg evidence confidence

🔹 C. Calibration Metrics (NOVEL ✅)

(How stable and realistic the confidence is?)

Raw score vs calibrated score delta

Overconfidence penalty rate

Bucket distribution (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW)

🔹 D. Explainability Metrics (VERY RARE 🔥)

Avg trace nodes per case

Avg evidence links per reasoning

% cases with full trace graph

🔹 E. Improvement vs Old Model (PAPER GOLD)

Baseline (old model):

Single heuristic score

No traceability

No calibration

New model:

Multi-agent reasoning

Trace graph

Calibration

We’ll compute relative improvement deltas.
